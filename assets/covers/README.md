Album and single covers for the yearbook's Fan Favorites page. Each one is printed on that
winner's record as a picture disc.

Name each file after the winner it belongs to, e.g. `go-hard.jpg` or `ddi-ro-ri.png`, or after a
category id (`song.png`, `mv.png`). PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG; square images look best. Restart the bot
after adding or changing covers. Winners without a cover get a printed sleeve with their title.
